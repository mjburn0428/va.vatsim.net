Audit requirements

Prepare the following for your current tier. Your Audit Manager will confirm whether each requirement is met.

  • Display the VATSIM logo on your website.
  • Provide evidence of at least 15 active pilots in the last 90 days and more than 50 flights on VATSIM.
  • Maintain a working website secured with HTTPS.
  • Make your privacy policy easily accessible.
  • Use secure, legitimate software that does not compromise the VATSIM network.
  • Maintain a working representative contact and respond to audit inquiries within 7–10 days.

How your audit works

1. Scheduled

Your VA enters its next audit cycle. Keep your public website, contact information and activity records current.

2. Audit in progress

An Audit Manager checks your VA against the program requirements. Respond to any requests the team sends you.

3. Department review

The department reviews the audit. It may return to the audit stage for corrections before a final outcome is recorded.

4. Completed

Read the outcome and download the final report when available. Follow up on any requested actions before the next audit.

What should I prepare?

Check that your VA's website or community invite works, your contact information is correct, and your activity and program information are up to date. Your audit team will explain any specific information they need.

When is my audit due?

An assigned audit is due 10 calendar days after its assignment date in AMS. The portal shows that assignment date and the calculated due date. Ask your Audit Manager in the audit conversation about any requested evidence or agreed deadlines.

My report or audit progress is missing

Older reports may need to be linked by the department. Contact the team through your usual department contact and include your VA name and the audit date. You can find the department team here.
